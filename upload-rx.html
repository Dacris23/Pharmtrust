Prescription Slip Requirements:
✓ Doctor / Prescriber Full Name & Signature
✓ Doctor's MDCN Folio Number & Clinic Stamp
✓ Date of Consultation (current and valid)
✕ Controlled psychotropics & scheduled narcotics (Tramadol, Codeine, Rohypnol) are rejected and will not be dispensed online.
